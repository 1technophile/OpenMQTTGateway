How it works

OpenMQTTGateway is a firmware for ESP32 and ESP8266 boards that translates your devices' signals into MQTT messages — and back. One board replaces a drawer full of proprietary bridges.

🌡️Your devicesBLE · RF · IR · LoRa
⇄
📟OpenMQTTGatewayESP32 / ESP8266
⇄
🔀MQTT brokerMosquitto...
⇄
🏠Your controllerHome Assistant, OpenHAB...

Decodes more than 100 BLE devices and many RF protocols (433/315/868/915MHz) through RTL_433 decoders — and the BLE decoding also runs on Raspberry Pi, Windows or Linux with Theengs Gateway.
